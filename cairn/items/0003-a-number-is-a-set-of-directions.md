The thesis milestone. A selector climbs to a level and hunts sideways for a free path; a connector climbs to the tens and steps to the units; the line at the end of that path rings. There is no table from numbers to telephones anywhere in the office. A subscriber's number is where their pair is soldered on the frame, and `./laporte spec` reports it as a result.

By the end, `./laporte call` renders a whole call heard from both ends: dial tone, the dialling, ringback, the bell, the answer, the hang-up — with no voice yet, because the voice is the next milestone.
