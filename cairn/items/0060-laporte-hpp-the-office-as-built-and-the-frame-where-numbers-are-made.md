## What it is

The office the spike decided on, wired. Subscribers' pairs are connected
to the connector banks on the frame: a list of which pair is soldered to
which bank terminal. That list is the only place a number exists in the
office, and it does not contain any numbers — only positions.

## What it must derive

Every subscriber's number, as a consequence of where their pair is.

- [ ] Moving one jumper on the frame changes exactly one subscriber's number
