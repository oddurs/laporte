A digit is not sent. A finger winds a spring, a governor lets it unwind at ten turns of a cam a second, and the cam breaks the current that many times. At the office, a magnet lifts a shaft one notch per break.

By the end of this milestone the dial is a mechanism, the first selector rises to the height of the digit dialled, and there is no `Digit` anywhere in the program. Tapping the switchhook five times dials five, and nobody wrote that.

Everything here can be heard: the line current, rendered as sound.
