The battery at the exchange powers your voice. A carbon transmitter is a resistor that a sound wave varies, and the current through it is the telephone signal; with no battery there is no voice at all.

This milestone builds the voice path — transmitter, receiver, the set's network that lets one pair of wires carry both directions, and the transmission bridge — and derives what a telephone sounds like rather than applying a filter that sounds like one. Sidetone, the faintness of a long loop and the shape of the response all fall out, or the model is wrong.
