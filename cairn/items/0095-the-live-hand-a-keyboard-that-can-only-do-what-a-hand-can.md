## What it is

Terminal keys as hand actions. Space lifts or replaces the handset; a
digit key pulls the dial to that hole and lets go — and the dial then
returns at the governor's speed, so you cannot dial faster than the
governor, and pressing a second digit while the first is still returning
does nothing, because your finger is on a moving wheel. `h` taps the hook.
`w` held whistles 2600 Hz — or the microphone does, if you can.
