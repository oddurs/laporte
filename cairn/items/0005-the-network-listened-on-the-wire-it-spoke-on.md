A second office, and a trunk between them. An idle trunk carries a 2600 Hz tone; a busy one is silent; dial pulses cross as bursts of tone. The signalling and the voice share one channel.

So a person who whistles 2600 Hz into the mouthpiece hangs up the far office, and a person who whistles it in pulses dials it. That is Joybubbles, and later the blue box, and none of it has any code: it is the consequence of signalling in-band, which this milestone builds honestly.
