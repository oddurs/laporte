## What it is

A 20 Hz alternating source of about 90 V rms (the ledger's figures),
superimposed on the office battery, switched onto a called line by the
connector in the cadence of the interrupter: two seconds on, four off.

The battery underneath the ringing is not an accident. It is what lets the
office notice an answer while it is still ringing.

## What it may touch

The connector's ringing contacts.
