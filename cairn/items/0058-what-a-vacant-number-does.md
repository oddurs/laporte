## The claim

Dialling a number whose bank position has no pair soldered to it does
whatever the wiring makes it do — and nobody chose what. The likely answer
is ringback forever into an open pair. The check records what happens; the
README states it, and says whether real offices of the era did the same
(to be sourced) or wired an intercept.
