## What it witnesses

The equipment, rather than the line. Every magnet stroke, every armature,
every shaft falling home.

## What it prints

A WAV of the switch room during a call: an impulse per mechanical event,
voiced by event type. The step-by-step office was famous for its sound; this
is the one place the program is allowed to be decorative, and it is
labelled as such.

## What it may not do

Invent an event. Each sound is a mechanical state change the tick recorded.
