## What it has to explain

The second claim, now earned: phreaking has no code. The publication of
the signalling frequencies; Joybubbles; the whistle in the cereal box;
and why the fix was not a better guard but moving the signalling out of
the voice channel altogether — common-channel signalling, from 1976 (to
be sourced) — which this project does not model and says so.

## What it must not do

Read as instructions for anything that still exists. It does not: no
network has signalled in-band like this for decades, and the prose says so.
