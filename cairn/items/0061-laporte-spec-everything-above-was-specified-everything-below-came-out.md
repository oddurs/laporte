## What it witnesses

The office as built.

## What it prints

Above the line: battery, cable, the switches installed, the frame's
jumpers by position. Below: each subscriber's number, found by asking
which digits would route a selector to their pair. Then the loop limit,
the derived dial tolerances, the release time — every derived figure in
one place.

## What it may not do

Read a number from anywhere it is stored, because it is not stored.
